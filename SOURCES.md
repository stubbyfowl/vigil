# Sources

Generated by `scripts/build_sources.py` from `data/sources.json` and `data/real_ranges.json`. Edit those files, not this one.

**Read** says how much of each source the team actually read. **Verified** means the figure was read in the source or its abstract. **Summary only** means it was read in a press release or a page quoting it, so check the original before quoting it to judges. Real people's individual records are never imported, even when they are public.
